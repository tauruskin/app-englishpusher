🏆 Present Perfect + Superlative

Lesson 4D — 25 questions · 8 cards

Gap Fill

It's the ___ film I've ever seen.

Answer: best

good→best, bad→worst, far→furthest — irregular, just like go→went.

This is the ___ hotel I've ever stayed in.

Answer: most expensive

expensive, beautiful, comfortable → most + adjective, never adjective+est.

That's the ___ mistake you've ever made.

Answer: worst

bad→worse→worst — the whole comparison chain is irregular.

She's the kindest person I ___ met.

Answer: have ever

have/has always comes before 'ever', and 'ever' always comes before the past participle.

This is the funniest joke I've ___ heard.

Answer: ever

ever = in your whole life so far — never, already and yet don't fit this meaning here.

This is the biggest company she ___ worked for.

Answer: has ever

I/we/you/they → have ever. He/she/it → has ever.

Error Spot

This is the goodest film I've ever seen.

Correction: best

'Good' is irregular: good→better→best. Never 'goodest'.

It's the baddest storm I've ever seen.

Correction: worst

'Bad' is irregular: the superlative is 'worst', not 'baddest'.

This is the biggest car I've ever drove.

Correction: driven

Present perfect needs the past participle: drive→drove→driven. Use 'driven' after 've.

He's the more intelligent person I've ever met.

Correction: most

This compares against everyone, so it needs the superlative 'most intelligent', not the comparative 'more intelligent'.

That's the worst mistake you've ever make.

Correction: made

Present perfect needs the past participle: make→made. Use 'made' after 've.

It's a most boring film I've ever seen.

Correction: the

Superlatives always take 'the': 'the most boring', never 'a most boring'.

Multiple Choice

Choose the sentence that correctly uses present perfect + superlative:

Answer: It's the best holiday I've ever had.

the + superlative + noun + subject + have/has + past participle — no past simple needed.

Choose the correct superlative form of 'far':

Answer: furthest

good→best, bad→worst, far→furthest/farthest — learn these by heart; they don't follow the normal rules.

Choose the sentence with 'ever' in the correct position:

Answer: She's the kindest manager I've ever worked for.

have + ever + past participle — never split 've' and 'ever', and never put 'ever' at the end.

Choose the correct sentence:

Answer: It's the most beautiful city I've ever visited.

'Ever' + a superlative always needs the present perfect: have/has + past participle.

Choose the correctly formed superlative:

Answer: This is the most exhausted I've ever been.

exhausted, expensive, beautiful, interesting → most + adjective, never adjective+est.

Choose the sentence with the correct past participle:

Answer: This is the silliest thing I've ever heard you say.

hear→heard, see→seen, eat→eaten — always the past participle after have/has.

Choose the sentence with the correct word order:

Answer: This is the biggest company I've ever worked for.

Build it in order: the → superlative → noun → subject → have/has → ever → past participle.

Word Transform

That's the ___ (funny) joke I've ever heard.

Answer: funniest

funny→funniest, easy→easiest, happy→happiest — change y to i before adding -est.

This is the ___ (bad) storm I've ever seen.

Answer: worst

bad→worst — the same irregular pattern as good→best and far→furthest.

Jake is the ___ (patient) manager I've ever had.

Answer: most patient

Two or more syllables, no -y ending → most + adjective.

I think this is the ___ (exhausted) I've ever been.

Answer: most exhausted

Past-participle adjectives (exhausted, bored, surprised) always use 'most', never '-est'.

It's probably the ___ (brilliant) film we've ever seen.

Answer: most brilliant

The longer the adjective, the more likely it needs 'most' instead of '-est'.

This is the ___ (far) I've ever travelled alone.

Answer: furthest

far→furthest (distance, figurative) or farthest (physical distance) — both are accepted.

Flashcards

the + superlative + present perfect

This is ___ (good) coffee I've had this week. as ___

Back: This is the best coffee I've had this week., answer as best

We use the present perfect with a superlative adjective to talk about the best, worst or most amazing experience of our life so far.

It's the biggest house I've seen. · That's the worst film I've watched.

«the + найвищий ступінь + Present Perfect» — найкращий/найгірший досвід «дотепер».

ever between have and the past participle

It's the nicest café I've ___ (be) to. as ___

Back: It's the nicest café I've ever been to., answer as ever been

'Ever' means 'at any time in your life'. It always goes between have/has and the past participle, never at the end of the sentence.

That's the funniest joke I've ever heard. · She's the kindest teacher I've ever had.

ever стоїть між have і дієприкметником: I've ever been, не I ever've been.

irregular superlatives: good→best, bad→worst, far→furthest

This is ___ (bad) storm I've seen this year. as ___

Back: This is the worst storm I've seen this year., answer as worst

Good, bad and far have irregular superlative forms: best, worst, furthest/farthest — never goodest or baddest.

This is the best meal I've ever eaten. · It's the furthest I've ever been from home.

good→best, bad→worst, far→furthest/farthest — не додавай -est.

short adjectives: add -est

This is ___ (long) book I've ever read. as ___

Back: This is the longest book I've ever read., answer as longest

Short, one-syllable adjectives take -est in the superlative: long→longest, old→oldest, fast→fastest.

It's the slowest car I've ever driven. · That's the coldest winter I've ever had.

короткі прикметники (1 склад): +est → longest, oldest, fastest.

adjectives ending in -y: -y → -iest

She's ___ (happy) person I've ever met. as ___

Back: She's the happiest person I've ever met., answer as happiest

Adjectives ending in a consonant + y change y to i and add -est: happy→happiest, easy→easiest, funny→funniest.

This is the easiest exam I've ever taken. · That's the funniest show I've ever watched.

прикметники на -y: y→i +est → happiest, easiest, funniest.

long adjectives: most + adjective

This is ___ (beautiful) place I've ever visited. as ___

Back: This is the most beautiful place I've ever visited., answer as most beautiful

Adjectives with two or more syllables (and not ending in -y) use 'most' + adjective: most expensive, most interesting, most beautiful.

This is the most comfortable sofa I've ever sat on. · It's the most interesting question you've asked me.

довгі прикметники: most + прикметник → most beautiful, most expensive.

past participle after have/has — not past simple

That's the most expensive bike I've ever ___ (buy). as ___

Back: That's the most expensive bike I've ever bought., answer as bought

After have/has in the present perfect, use the past participle, not the past simple or base form: buy→bought, not buyed or buy.

It's the best meal I've ever eaten. · This is the biggest mistake I've ever made at work.

після have/has — дієприкметник (bought), не Past Simple чи базова форма.

'the' before the superlative adjective

That's ___ nicest present I've ever received. as ___

Back: That's the nicest present I've ever received., answer as the

Superlative adjectives always need 'the' in front of them — never 'a' or no article at all.

She's the funniest comedian I've ever seen live. · It's the tallest building I've ever been inside.

перед найвищим ступенем завжди 'the': the best, не a best.
