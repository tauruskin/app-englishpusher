💪 Can, Could, Be Able To

Lesson 8A — 25 questions · 8 cards

Gap Fill

My grandfather ___ fix almost anything when he was younger.

Answer: could

could = general ability in the past. Look for time expressions like 'when he was younger'.

These days, she ___ speak four languages.

Answer: can

can = ability now. could / was able to = ability in the past.

After trying for an hour, we finally ___ open the heavy door.

Answer: were able to

A single past success = was/were able to. 'Could' is wrong here.

I ___ remember his name, even though we'd met before.

Answer: couldn't

couldn't = past inability — works for both a general inability and a single occasion.

We ___ offer you a discount today only.

Answer: are able to

are able to ≈ can, even for an offer available at one specific time.

___ you swim when you were a child?

Answer: Could

Could you...? asks about a general past ability.

Error Spot

She can plays the piano beautifully.

Correction: play

After 'can', use the base form: 'can play', not 'can plays'.

He is able to playing the guitar well.

Correction: play

After 'able to', use the base form: 'able to play', not 'able to playing'.

They was able to finish the race in record time.

Correction: were

'They' needs the plural past form: 'They were able to...', not 'They was able to...'.

When I was young, I can't ride a bicycle.

Correction: couldn't

The sentence is about childhood, so use the past form: 'I couldn't ride', not 'I can't ride'.

Was you able to drive before you moved here?

Correction: Were

'You' needs 'were': 'Were you able to...?', not 'Was you able to...?'.

I couldn't speaking any English when I arrived.

Correction: speak

After 'couldn't', use the base form: 'couldn't speak', not 'couldn't speaking'.

Multiple Choice

Which sentence is correct?

Answer: She can play the violin beautifully.

can + base verb, no 'to': can play, can swim, can cook.

Which sentence is correct?

Answer: I could run very fast when I was a teenager.

could = general ability in the past — look for past time expressions.

Which sentence means the same thing?

Answer: We are able to repair the roof this afternoon.

can ≈ be able to in the present, for general ability AND a specific time.

Which sentence is grammatically correct?

Answer: After a difficult exam, she was able to pass with a high grade.

A single past success = was/were able to. 'Could' is wrong here, even though it's fine for general ability.

Which sentence has the same meaning?

Answer: I couldn't sleep last night.

Negative past ability: couldn't = wasn't/weren't able to — always interchangeable.

Which question is correct?

Answer: Were you able to find a parking space near the station?

Were + subject + able to + base verb — don't add 'did' or 'can' as well.

Which sentence is correct?

Answer: When the lift broke down, we were able to get out through a window.

A dramatic one-time escape or success in the past always needs was/were able to.

Word Transform

He ___ (be) able to speak Japanese fluently.

Answer: is

can = is/am/are able to (present ability, same meaning).

I ___ (be) able to type very fast.

Answer: am

I can / I am able to — the same meaning, different grammar.

They ___ (be) able to finish the marathon together.

Answer: were

could = was/were able to (general ability in the past).

After a lot of effort, she finally ___ (be) able to open the stuck window.

Answer: was

'could' is WRONG for a single past achievement — use was/were able to instead.

He ___ (be) able to attend the meeting because he's abroad this week.

Answer: isn't

can't = isn't/aren't able to (present inability, same meaning).

We ___ (be) able to contact her yesterday because her phone was off.

Answer: weren't

couldn't = wasn't/weren't able to — both work for a single past occasion too.

Flashcards

can — present ability (general)

My sister ___ speak Spanish really well.

Back: My sister can speak Spanish really well., answer as can

Can describes a general ability in the present. We use can + the base form of the verb, with no 'to'.

He can cook amazing Italian food. · We can start the meeting whenever you're ready.

can = «можу» (здатність зараз); після can дієслово без to.

can't — present inability

He ___ swim, so he's having lessons this summer.

Back: He can't swim, so he's having lessons this summer., answer as can't

Can't is the negative of can. It shows someone is unable to do something now, or as a general ability.

I can't drive — I never learned. · She can't come to the party tonight.

can't = «не можу» (зараз, загалом).

could — past ability (general)

When I was ten, I ___ climb trees really quickly.

Back: When I was ten, I could climb trees really quickly., answer as could

Could describes a general ability someone had in the past, over a period of time — not one single occasion.

My grandmother could speak four languages. · We could see the mountains from our old house.

could = «міг/могла» — загальна здатність у минулому, не одноразова подія.

couldn't — past inability

I ___ understand the instructions, so I asked for help.

Back: I couldn't understand the instructions, so I asked for help., answer as couldn't

Couldn't is the negative of could. Unlike could, it can describe BOTH a general past inability AND a single occasion in the past.

He couldn't swim when he was a child. · We couldn't find the restaurant last night.

couldn't = «не міг» — і загалом, і в один конкретний момент.

be able to — present ability

She ___ (be) able to fix almost any computer problem.

Back: She is able to fix almost any computer problem., answer as is

Be able to means the same as can for present ability. It changes form like a normal verb: I am able to, she is able to, they are able to.

We are able to help you with that today. · I'm not able to come to the meeting this morning.

be able to ≈ can (зараз); змінюється як звичайне дієслово: is/am/are able to.

was/were able to — past ability (general)

My parents ___ (be) able to speak three languages between them.

Back: My parents were able to speak three languages between them., answer as were

Was/were able to means the same as could for a general ability in the past.

He was able to read before he started school. · They were able to ski really well as children.

was/were able to ≈ could (загальна здатність у минулому).

was/were able to — one specific past success (not could)

The car broke down, but we ___ (be) able to fix it ourselves.

Back: The car broke down, but we were able to fix it ourselves., answer as were

For ONE specific achievement in the past — something that happened on one occasion — we must use was/were able to. We do NOT use could here.

After a long search, she was able to find her lost ring. · The team was able to win the final despite the rain.

для однієї конкретної перемоги в минулому — тільки was/were able to, НЕ could.

questions: Can / Could / Were ... able to

___ you able to finish the report on time yesterday?

Back: Were you able to finish the report on time yesterday., answer as Were

In questions, we ask Can...? for the present, Could...? for a general past ability, and Were you able to...? especially for one specific past occasion.

Can you speak German? · Could you swim when you were young?

питання: Can...? (зараз), Could...? (загалом у минулому), Were able to...? (один випадок).
