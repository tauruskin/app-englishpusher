Gap Fill

Answer: She gave me a book.

Word Transform

She ___ (send) me a postcard from Spain last month.

Answer: sent

send → sent (irregular past). Pattern 1: verb + indirect object + direct object, no preposition needed.

He ___ (lend) his brother some money yesterday.

Answer: lent

lend → lent (irregular past). Don't confuse 'lend' (give temporarily) with 'borrow' (receive temporarily).

They ___ (give) their neighbours a copy of the key last week.

Answer: gave

give → gave (irregular past). Pattern 1 works well when the direct object is short.

I ___ (buy) my sister a scarf for her birthday.

Answer: bought

buy → bought (irregular past). Remember: 'buy' takes 'for' in pattern 2, never 'to'.

We ___ (show) the tourists the way to the station this morning.

Answer: showed

show → showed (regular past). 'Show' takes 'to' in pattern 2: 'showed the way to the tourists'.

She ___ (teach) her daughter to ride a bike last summer.

Answer: taught

teach → taught (irregular past). 'Teach' takes 'to' in pattern 2: 'taught Spanish to them'.
