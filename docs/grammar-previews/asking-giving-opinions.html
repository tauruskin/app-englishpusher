💭 Asking & Giving Opinions

Unit 6C — 27 questions · 9 cards

Gap Fill

What do you think ___ the new restaurant downtown?

Answer: of

'What do you think of...?' always takes 'of'.

___ me, the film was far too long and quite boring.

Answer: For

'For me, ...' = a personal opinion.

In my ___, graffiti is art, not vandalism.

Answer: view

'In my view, ...' is a slightly more formal way to give an opinion.

I ___ say that the sculpture looks amazing at night.

Answer: would

'I would say (that)...' sounds more careful than a flat statement.

"The acting in that film was brilliant." "What makes you ___ that?"

Answer: say

'What makes you say that?' = 'Why do you think that?'

I know you disagree with me, but I ___ we're all different.

Answer: suppose

'I suppose/guess we're all different' ends a disagreement politely.

Error Spot

What do you think about the new art gallery?

Correction: of

Think of + noun (opinion), not 'think about' in this fixed phrase.

For I, the sculpture looks amazing in the evening light.

Correction: me,

'For me, ...' — always the object pronoun 'me', never 'I'.

Why does you think that?

Correction: do

'Why do you think that?' — 'do', not 'does', with 'you'.

It's because the actors was brilliant.

Correction: were

Plural subject + 'were', not 'was'.

The reason is because I don't like modern art.

Correction: that

'The reason is that...', never 'the reason is because...'.

The exhibition was very fantastic, with huge sculptures everywhere.

Correction: absolutely

very + normal adjective; absolutely + extreme adjective.

Multiple Choice

You want to ask a friend what they think about a new café. Which question is correct?

Answer: What do you think of the café?

'What do you think of...?' — don't drop 'do' or change the preposition.

Choose the extreme adjective for 'good'.

Answer: fantastic

good → fantastic/outstanding (extreme pair).

Choose the extreme adjective for 'small'.

Answer: tiny

small → tiny (extreme pair).

Choose the extreme adjective for 'difficult'.

Answer: impossible

difficult → impossible (extreme pair).

Choose the correct sentence.

Answer: The film was absolutely fantastic.

very + normal adjective; absolutely + extreme adjective.

Someone asks you 'Why do you think that?' How can you reply, giving a reason?

Answer: It's because the music was too loud.

'It's because...' and 'The reason is...' both give a reason.

Two friends have completely different opinions about a film. Which phrase politely ends the disagreement?

Answer: I guess we all like different things.

'I suppose we're all different' and 'I guess we all like different things' end a disagreement politely.

Word Transform

What ___ (do/did) you think of the concert last night?

Answer: did

'What did you think of...?' — for a finished event.

___ (Would/Will) you say the ending was happy or sad?

Answer: Would

'Would you say (that)...?' invites a thoughtful opinion.

These days, I ___ (think/thought) that modern art is more interesting than old paintings.

Answer: think

'I think...' = a current opinion; 'I thought...' = a past one.

It's ___ (because/so) the service was so slow that I didn't enjoy the meal.

Answer: because

'It's because + reason' explains why.

The ___ (reason/cause) is that I prefer books with a happy ending.

Answer: reason

'The reason is (that) + explanation'.

I guess we all like different ___ (things/thing).

Answer: things

'I guess we all like different things' ends a disagreement politely.

Sentence Match

Match each beginning to its correct ending.

  • What do you think → of the meal?
  • The reason is → the traffic was terrible.
  • I guess we all like → different things.
  • What makes you → say that?

Pay attention to the exact collocation each phrase needs (think OF, like different THINGS...).

Match each beginning to its correct ending.

  • How about → you?
  • I guess it's good we're → not all the same.
  • It was absolutely → huge.
  • It was very → small.

'Absolutely' goes with extreme adjectives (huge); 'very' goes with normal adjectives (small).

Flashcards

What do you think of...?

What do you think ___ the new art gallery?

Back: What do you think of the new art gallery?, answer as of

Use 'What do/did you think of + noun' to ask for someone's opinion. Use 'did' for something in the past.

What do you think of this restaurant? · What did you think of the concert last night?

«Що ти думаєш про...?» — think of, не think about.

For me, ... (giving an opinion)

___ me, the sculpture looks fantastic in the evening light.

Back: For me, the sculpture looks fantastic in the evening light., answer as For

'For me' introduces a personal opinion, similar to 'in my opinion'. It emphasises that this is your own view.

For me, this is the best album of the year. · For me, graffiti is art, not vandalism.

«Для мене» (особиста думка): For me, ... = «на мою думку».

In my view, ... (giving an opinion)

___ my view, the film was far too long.

Back: In my view, the film was far too long., answer as In

'In my view' is a fixed phrase for giving an opinion, similar to 'I think'. It usually starts the sentence and is followed by a comma.

In my view, public art should be free for everyone. · In my view, the new app is much easier to use.

«На мою думку» — In my view, (фіксована фраза, кома після).

I would say (that)...

I ___ say that modern art is more interesting than old paintings.

Back: I would say that modern art is more interesting than old paintings., answer as would

'I would say' is a slightly careful, polite way to give an opinion, especially when the topic is debatable.

I would say the film was OK, but not great. · I would say she's one of the best photographers today.

I would say — обережний спосіб висловити думку: «я б сказав, що...».

Why do you think that? (asking for a reason)

"I loved the performance." "___ do you think that?"

Back: "I loved the performance." "Why do you think that?", answer as Why

Use 'Why do you think that?' to ask someone for the reason behind their opinion.

"This design is amazing." "Why do you think that?" · "I didn't enjoy the book." "Why do you think that?"

«Чому ти так думаєш?» — Why do you think that?

It's because... (giving a reason)

I loved the show. It's ___ the actors were brilliant.

Back: I loved the show. It's because the actors were brilliant., answer as because

Use 'it's because' to give the reason for your opinion. 'Because' introduces the cause.

I didn't like the ending. It's because it was too sad. · She enjoys the gym. It's because it helps her relax.

«Це тому що» — It's because + причина твоєї думки.

I suppose we're all different (agreeing to disagree)

You hated the film, but I loved it. I ___ we're all different.

Back: You hated the film, but I loved it. I suppose we're all different., answer as suppose

Use 'I suppose we're all different' or 'I guess we all like different things' to politely end a disagreement about opinions.

I guess we all like different things. · I suppose we're all different — my sister hates spicy food too.

«Мабуть, ми всі різні» — ввічливо завершити суперечку про смаки.

very + normal adjective, absolutely + extreme adjective

The film wasn't just good, it was ___ (very / absolutely) fantastic!

Back: The film wasn't just good, it was absolutely fantastic!, answer as absolutely

Extreme adjectives like fantastic, terrible and huge already have a very strong meaning, so we use 'absolutely', not 'very', to make them stronger.

The meal was absolutely delicious. · It was a very good film, but not absolutely amazing.

extreme прикметники (fantastic, terrible) підсилюємо absolutely, а не very.

extreme adjectives (bad → terrible, good → fantastic)

The food wasn't just bad, it was absolutely ___ (terrible / awful).

Back: The food wasn't just bad, it was absolutely terrible., answer as terrible

Extreme adjectives like terrible, fantastic, huge and tiny have a stronger meaning than normal adjectives like bad, good, big and small.

The new phone is tiny — it fits in any pocket. · The sculpture is huge; you can see it from the other side of the park.

extreme прикметники (terrible, fantastic, huge, tiny) сильніші за звичайні (bad, good, big, small).
