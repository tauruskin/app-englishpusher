👜 Possessive Pronouns

Lesson 5A — 25 questions · 7 cards

Gap Fill

This isn't your umbrella. I think it's ___.

Answer: mine

mine = my + noun. Never put a noun straight after mine.

We left our bags in the car, but Tom and Ana left ___ at the hotel.

Answer: theirs

theirs = their + noun, used alone to avoid repeating the noun.

I can't find my charger. Can I borrow ___?

Answer: hers

hers = her + noun. Compare: her charger → hers.

My sister and I share a flat. ___ is on the third floor.

Answer: ours

ours = our + noun, used without repeating the noun.

I found two umbrellas at the office. Is one of them ___?

Answer: yours

yours = your + noun. Never say 'yours umbrella'.

He says this isn't his jacket, but I'm sure it's ___.

Answer: his

his never changes form — his jacket (adjective) and it's his (pronoun) look identical.

Error Spot

This bag isn't yours, it's my.

Correction: mine

No noun follows, so use the possessive pronoun: 'This bag isn't yours, it's mine.'

Can I borrow your pen? I can't find mine pen.

Correction: my

'Pen' follows, so use the adjective: 'I can't find my pen.'

Whose jacket is this? I think it's her.

Correction: hers

No noun follows, so use the possessive pronoun: 'I think it's hers.'

That car outside is ours car.

Correction: our

'Car' follows, so use the adjective: 'That car outside is our car.'

We didn't bring our umbrellas, so we used theirs umbrellas.

Correction: their

'Umbrellas' follows, so use the adjective: 'We used their umbrellas.'

This isn't your seat, it's him.

Correction: his

This shows possession, so use the possessive pronoun: 'This isn't your seat, it's his.'

Multiple Choice

Which sentence is correct?

Answer: This is mine.

mine on its own, never 'mine' + noun.

Which sentence is correct?

Answer: That jacket is hers.

hers has no apostrophe and no noun after it.

A: Whose umbrella is this? B: ___

Answer: It's mine.

Whose is this? → It's mine / yours / his / hers / ours / theirs.

Which sentence is correct?

Answer: Our flat is lovely.

our + noun (our flat). ours alone, no noun, no apostrophe.

Which sentence is correct?

Answer: The company increased its profits.

its = possession, no apostrophe. it's = it is / it has, with an apostrophe.

A: Is this bag yours? B: ___

Answer: No, it's not mine.

it's not mine — short, no noun, no extra word after mine.

Which sentence is correct?

Answer: This isn't our car — it's theirs.

theirs — no apostrophe, no noun after it.

Word Transform

This umbrella isn't yours, it's ___ (I).

Answer: mine

I → my (adjective) → mine (pronoun, no noun after it).

That dog isn't our dog. It's ___ (they).

Answer: theirs

they → their (adjective) → theirs (pronoun, no noun after it).

I like your idea, but I prefer ___ (we).

Answer: ours

we → our (adjective) → ours (pronoun, no noun after it).

He found a wallet. He thinks it's ___ (she).

Answer: hers

she → her (adjective) → hers (pronoun, no apostrophe).

This seat is taken. I think it's ___ (he).

Answer: his

he → his (adjective) → his (pronoun — same spelling).

We don't have keys like that. Those must be ___ (you).

Answer: yours

you → your (adjective) → yours (pronoun, singular or plural 'you').

Flashcards

mine = my + noun (no noun after the pronoun)

This isn't your coat — it's ___ (I).

Back: This isn't your coat — it's mine.

'Mine' replaces 'my' + noun. A possessive pronoun stands alone — we never put a noun straight after it.

I can't find my keys. Have you seen mine? · The red bike is mine, not my brother's.

mine = «мій/моя/моє», але без іменника після: my coat → mine. Не можна сказати «mine coat» — іменник після займенника не ставимо.

yours = your + noun

I left my umbrella at home. Can I borrow ___ (you)?

Back: I left my umbrella at home. Can I borrow yours?

'Yours' replaces 'your' + noun ('your umbrella'). It works the same way for one person or several people.

Is this pen yours? · Our garden is small, but yours is huge!

yours = «твій/твоя/твоє» або «ваш/ваша/ваше», без іменника: your umbrella → yours.

his — same word for adjective and pronoun

That's not my phone — I think it's ___ (he).

Back: That's not my phone — I think it's his.

'His' is unusual: it is both the possessive adjective ('his phone') and the possessive pronoun ('it's his'). The spelling never changes.

His car is the blue one. · This jacket isn't mine — it's his.

his — єдина форма і для прикметника (his phone — «його телефон»), і для займенника (it's his — «це його»). Форма не змінюється.

hers = her + noun

This scarf isn't mine. I think it's ___ (she).

Back: This scarf isn't mine. I think it's hers.

'Hers' replaces 'her' + noun ('her scarf'). There is never an apostrophe — 'her's' doesn't exist.

That umbrella is hers, not mine. · I like your bag more than hers.

hers = «її», без іменника: her scarf → hers. Апострофа немає: не «her's», а hers.

ours = our + noun

Your flat is on the second floor. ___ (we) is on the fifth.

Back: Your flat is on the second floor. Ours is on the fifth.

'Ours' replaces 'our' + noun ('our flat'). It refers to something belonging to the speaker and at least one other person.

This table is ours, not theirs. · Their garden is bigger than ours.

ours = «наш/наша/наше», без іменника: our flat → ours.

theirs = their + noun

We brought our own chairs. They brought ___ (they) too.

Back: We brought our own chairs. They brought theirs too.

'Theirs' replaces 'their' + noun ('their chairs'). It refers to something belonging to two or more other people.

Our team lost, but theirs won. · Is that suitcase theirs?

theirs = «їхній/їхня/їхнє», без іменника: their chairs → theirs.

its vs it's — no apostrophe in the possessive

The company increased ___ (it) profits this year.

Back: The company increased its profits this year.

'Its' (no apostrophe) shows possession — the profits belonging to the company. 'It's' (with an apostrophe) is short for 'it is' or 'it has' — a completely different word.

The dog wagged its tail. · It's going to rain later.

its (без апострофа) — присвійна форма «його/її» про предмет: its tail. it's (з апострофом) = it is / it has — зовсім інше слово.
