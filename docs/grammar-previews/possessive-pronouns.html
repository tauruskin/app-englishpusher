Possessive Pronouns

Lesson 5A — 25 questions

Gap Fill

I bought this umbrella myself, so it's ___.

Answer: mine

I → my (+ noun) → mine (no noun). Say 'this is mine', never 'this is mine umbrella'.

A: Is this your phone? B: Yes, it's ___.

Answer: yours

You → your (+ noun) → yours (no noun, no apostrophe!).

Daniel left his jacket at my house, so I gave it back — it was ___.

Answer: his

His jacket = adjective (+ noun). It was his = pronoun (no noun). 'His' never changes form.

Maria left her umbrella on the table, so the red umbrella there is ___.

Answer: hers

She → her (+ noun) → hers (no noun). Never write 'her's'.

We reserved table 12, so the reservation for table 12 is ___.

Answer: ours

We → our (+ noun) → ours (no noun). Never write 'our's'.

The Smiths own the house on the corner, so the house on the corner is ___.

Answer: theirs

They → their (+ noun) → theirs (no noun). Never write 'their's'.

Error Spot

That jacket is her.

Correction: hers

'Her' is an object pronoun (I saw her). To show possession alone, use 'hers': 'That jacket is hers.'

This bag isn't my — it's yours.

Correction: mine

Possessive pronouns stand alone with no noun — use 'mine', not 'my': 'This bag isn't mine.'

This is mine book, not yours.

Correction: my

A possessive pronoun can't be followed by a noun — use 'my' + 'book': 'This is my book.'

The blue car is theirs car, not ours.

Correction: their

A possessive pronoun can't be followed by a noun — use 'their' + 'car': 'The blue car is their car.'

Is this umbrella your's?

Correction: yours

'Yours' never takes an apostrophe: 'Is this umbrella yours?'

We found our keys, but they couldn't find theirs's.

Correction: theirs

'Theirs' is complete on its own — never add an apostrophe + s: '...but they couldn't find theirs.'

Multiple Choice

Which sentence is correct?

Answer: This phone is mine.

After 'is/are' with no noun following, use the possessive pronoun, not the adjective.

Which word means 'belonging to us'?

Answer: ours

We → our (+ noun) → ours (no noun).

Which sentence correctly uses a possessive pronoun?

Answer: That is hers.

Her bag → hers. Never 'hers bag'.

Which sentence is correct?

Answer: Ours is the red car.

If a noun follows, use the adjective (our car); if not, use the pronoun (ours).

Which sentence is correct?

Answer: Theirs is the house on the corner.

Theirs = belongs to them. There's = there is. Never write 'their's'.

Which sentence is correct?

Answer: Is this bag yours?

Yours, his, hers, ours, theirs — none of them ever take an apostrophe.

Which sentence is correct?

Answer: Hers is red; mine is blue.

Once the noun (car) is established, switch to the possessive pronoun: hers, mine.

Word Transform

This seat is taken, but the one next to it is ___ (I).

Answer: mine

I → mine. Never 'my' alone without a noun.

Is this umbrella ___ (you)?

Answer: yours

You → yours. No apostrophe — 'your's' is always wrong.

My keys are on the table. Where are ___ (he)?

Answer: his

He → his, whether or not a noun follows.

Our team won, but ___ (they) lost.

Answer: theirs

They → theirs. Never add an apostrophe: not 'their's'.

She has her own car; this blue one is ___ (she).

Answer: hers

She → hers. Never 'her's' — that form doesn't exist.

We all brought packed lunches, but ___ (we) was the biggest.

Answer: ours

We → ours. Never 'our's'.
