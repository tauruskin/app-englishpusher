📣 Making & Responding to Recommendations

Unit 7C — 27 questions · 10 cards

Gap Fill

There's a night market in the old town that you absolutely have ___ visit.

Answer: to

You absolutely have to + base verb — a really strong recommendation.

You ___ visit the old quarter. It's beautiful at night.

Answer: must

You must + base verb — no 'to' after must.

You should ___ try the seafood market. Everyone recommends it.

Answer: definitely

Should definitely + base verb — a stronger version of should.

Make sure ___ take comfortable shoes. You'll be walking a lot.

Answer: you

Make sure you + base verb — a reminder about something important.

Visiting the night market is one of the best things ___ do in this city.

Answer: to

One of the best things to do — always with 'to'.

Don't leave the island without ___ the famous sunset cliffs.

Answer: seeing

Don't leave without + -ing — a very strong 'you must do this' recommendation.

Error Spot

You must visiting the cathedral. It's amazing.

Correction: visit

Must + base verb: must visit, must see, must try.

There are a few things you absolutely has to see here.

Correction: have

You/I/we/they have to... He/she/it has to...

You should definite visit the botanical gardens while you're in town.

Correction: definitely

Definite is an adjective (describes a noun). Definitely is an adverb (describes a verb).

Make sure your pack extra water for the hike.

Correction: you

Make sure you + base verb — 'you' is the subject, not 'your'.

Trying the local street food are one of the best things to do here.

Correction: is

A gerund subject (Trying..., Visiting..., Walking...) is always singular: it takes 'is'.

Don't leave the city without see the night market. It's unforgettable.

Correction: seeing

Without + -ing: without trying, without seeing, without visiting.

Multiple Choice

Which sentence makes the strongest recommendation to visit a place?

Answer: You must visit the temple.

must > should definitely > could, for recommendation strength.

A friend recommends a new café you haven't tried yet. Which response politely shows interest?

Answer: That sounds interesting.

That sounds + adjective = a polite, flexible response to any new recommendation.

Which phrase correctly completes: 'Make sure ___ a hat — it's very sunny today.'?

Answer: you bring

Make sure you + base verb: bring, take, check, book.

Which sentence correctly uses the pattern 'one of the best things to do'?

Answer: Climbing the hill is one of the best things to do here.

One of the best things to do — don't drop 'the', the plural -s, or 'to'.

Your friend tells you some exciting travel news. Which response shows the most enthusiasm?

Answer: How exciting!

How + adjective! — stress the adjective to sound enthusiastic.

Which sentence correctly recommends an activity using 'It's a great place to...'?

Answer: It's a great place to walk along the river.

It's a great place to + verb: to see, to eat, to relax.

Which sentence correctly uses 'Don't leave without...' to give a strong recommendation?

Answer: Don't leave without trying the local cheese.

Don't leave without + -ing — never 'without' + base verb or 'to' + verb.

Word Transform

You should ___ (definite) try the floating market before you leave.

Answer: definitely

Definite → definitely. An adjective describes a noun; an adverb describes a verb.

Don't leave the city without ___ (try) the famous dumplings.

Answer: trying

Without + -ing: without trying, without seeing, without visiting.

Walking along the old city walls is one of the best things ___ (do) in this town.

Answer: to do

One of the best things to do/see/try — always with 'to'.

It's a lovely place ___ (watch) the sunset over the harbour.

Answer: to watch

It's a great place to + verb: to see, to eat, to relax.

Make sure you ___ (bring) a reusable water bottle. It gets very hot here.

Answer: bring

Make sure you + base verb: bring, take, check, book.

A: I'm going whale watching tomorrow! B: Oh wow! How ___ (excite)!

Answer: exciting

Excite (verb) → exciting (adjective). How exciting! How interesting! How amazing!

Flashcards

have to + base verb (strong recommendation)

There are so many things you absolutely have ___ try during a trip to Thailand - the street food is incredible.

Back: There are so many things you absolutely have to try during a trip to Thailand - the street food is incredible. Answer as to

Have to + base verb makes a strong, enthusiastic recommendation. The structure is have + to + base verb, and it doesn't change for different subjects.

You absolutely have to see the floating markets. · There's a beach you have to visit - it's stunning.

have to + дієслово - «обов'язково треба» (сильна порада).

must + base verb (no 'to')

You must ___ (to see / see) the cathedral - it's stunning inside.

Back: You must see the cathedral - it's stunning inside. Answer as see

After 'must', use the base verb with no 'to'. 'Must to see' is incorrect.

You must try the local cheese. · You must visit the museum before you leave.

must + дієслово без to: «мусиш побачити» - you must see, не must to see.

should definitely + base verb

You should ___ visit the night market before you go home.

Back: You should definitely visit the night market before you go home. Answer as definitely

'Should definitely' makes a recommendation stronger than plain 'should'. The adverb goes between 'should' and the main verb.

You should definitely try the street food. · You should definitely watch the sunset from the bridge.

should definitely + дієслово - сильніша порада, ніж просто should.

Make sure you + base verb

Make sure ___ take a map with you - the streets are confusing.

Back: Make sure you take a map with you - the streets are confusing. Answer as you

'Make sure you' + base verb reminds someone to do something important. Don't forget 'you' after 'sure'.

Make sure you book the tickets in advance. · Make sure you try the coffee - it's famous here.

Make sure you + дієслово - «обов'язково зроби...» (нагадування).

one of the best things to do

Walking along the river at sunset is one of the best things ___ do here.

Back: Walking along the river at sunset is one of the best things to do here. Answer as to

'One of the best things to do' + infinitive with 'to' describes a top recommendation among several good options.

Visiting the old castle is one of the best things to do in the city. · Trying the local market is one of the best things to do here.

one of the best things to do - «одна з найкращих речей» (топ-рекомендація).

It's a great/lovely place to + base verb

It's a great place ___ watch the sunset over the bay.

Back: It's a great place to watch the sunset over the bay. Answer as to

'It's a great/lovely place to' + base verb recommends an activity you can do at a specific place.

It's a lovely place to relax after a long day. · It's a great place to take photos of the skyline.

It's a great/lovely place to + дієслово - рекомендація, що там варто робити.

Don't leave without + -ing

Don't leave the city without ___ (try) the famous street food.

Back: Don't leave the city without trying the famous street food. Answer as trying

'Don't leave without' + -ing form strongly recommends something you must not miss. Use the gerund after 'without'.

Don't leave without visiting the botanical gardens. · Don't leave without seeing the view from the tower.

Don't leave without + -ing - «не їдь, не...» (обов'язково зроби перед від'їздом).

responding: That sounds + adjective

A: You should visit the floating market. B: That ___ interesting!

Back: A: You should visit the floating market. B: That sounds interesting! Answer as sounds

Use 'That sounds' + adjective to respond positively to a recommendation you haven't tried yet.

That sounds like a great idea. · That sounds amazing - let's go there.

That sounds + прикметник - реакція на пораду: «Звучить цікаво!».

responding: That's a good idea

A: Make sure you book early. B: OK, that's a good ___.

Back: A: Make sure you book early. B: OK, that's a good idea. Answer as idea

'That's a good idea' is a simple, friendly way to accept a recommendation or suggestion.

That's a good idea - I'll do that. · That's a good idea, thanks for telling me.

That's a good idea - проста фраза, щоб погодитися з порадою.

responding: How + adjective! (enthusiasm)

A: I'm going to Shanghai next month. B: Oh wow! How ___!

Back: A: I'm going to Shanghai next month. B: Oh wow! How exciting! Answer as exciting

'How' + adjective! is an enthusiastic reaction to good news. Stress the adjective to sound more enthusiastic.

How wonderful! I can't wait to hear about it. · How amazing! You'll love the city.

How + прикметник! - емоційна реакція: «Як чудово!», «Як цікаво!».
