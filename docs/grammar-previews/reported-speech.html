🗣️ Reported Speech

Lesson 5B · 25 questions · 10 cards

Gap Fill

He said that he ___ in Madrid.

Answer: lived

Reporting verb in the past (said/told) → shift the original verb one tense back.

They said that they ___ a documentary.

Answer: were watching

Present continuous (is/am/are + -ing) → past continuous (was/were + -ing) in reported speech.

She told me that she ___ the report.

Answer: had finished

Present perfect (have/has + past participle) → past perfect (had + past participle) in reported speech.

They said that they ___ that film the week before.

Answer: had seen

Past simple can backshift to past perfect in reported speech, especially when it happened before something else.

He told us that he ___ three languages.

Answer: could speak

can → could in reported speech, just like will → would.

She said that she ___ me back later.

Answer: would call

will → would in reported speech. The pronoun also changes: 'you' (the listener) becomes 'me'.

Error Spot

She told to me that she needed help.

Correction: said

'Told' is never followed by 'to'. Either say 'told me' or 'said to me' — changing 'told' to 'said' fixes the sentence.

He said me that he would be late.

Correction: told

'Say' cannot take a person directly after it — change 'said' to 'told': 'He told me...'.

She said that she lives in Berlin.

Correction: lived

The original statement ('I live in Berlin') is present simple, so it must backshift to past simple: lived.

He told us that he will help us.

Correction: would

'Will' backshifts to 'would' in reported speech: 'He told us that he would help us.'

She said that she can drive a truck.

Correction: could

'Can' backshifts to 'could' in reported speech: 'She said that she could drive a truck.'

He said that my flight was delayed.

Correction: his

He was talking about his own flight, so the possessive must match him: 'his flight', not 'my flight'.

Multiple Choice

Which sentence correctly reports what Anna said?

Answer: Anna said that she was tired.

be (am/is/are) → was/were in reported speech, just like any other present simple verb.

Which sentence correctly reports what Tom said?

Answer: Tom said that he had to leave early.

have to / has to → had to in reported speech, no matter who is speaking.

Which sentence correctly reports what the neighbours said?

Answer: Our neighbours said that they were going to paint the fence this weekend.

am/is/are going to → was/were going to. The rest of the phrase ('going to paint') stays the same.

Which sentence uses the reporting verb correctly?

Answer: David told me that he was busy.

tell + person (told me) vs say (to) + person (said to me) — never 'told to' or 'said me'.

Which sentence correctly reports what Laura said?

Answer: Laura said that she had lost her keys.

present perfect → past perfect, and don't forget the possessive change: my → her.

Which sentence correctly reports what they told you?

Answer: They told us that they had missed the bus.

past simple → past perfect in reported speech, especially for something that happened earlier.

Which sentence correctly reports what Tom said to you?

Answer: Tom said that he couldn't finish his project without my help.

Check every word that depends on who is speaking: the modal verb AND every pronoun and possessive.

Word Transform

She said that she ___ (feel) great.

Answer: felt

Present simple always backshifts to past simple after said/told.

They told us that they ___ (enjoy) the trip.

Answer: were enjoying

Present continuous (is/am/are + -ing) → past continuous (was/were + -ing).

He said that he ___ (finish) his homework.

Answer: had finished

Present perfect → past perfect in reported speech.

She told me that she ___ (see) Anna at the station.

Answer: had seen

Past simple can backshift to past perfect in reported speech.

He said that he ___ (have to) work this weekend.

Answer: had to

have to / has to → had to, whoever is speaking.

He told his manager that he ___ (be) going to resign.

Answer: was

am/is/are going to → was/were going to — 'going to' itself never changes.

Flashcards

say vs tell

Mark ___ (tell) his boss that he was running late.

Back: Mark told his boss that he was running late., answer as told

'Tell' always needs a person straight after it: tell someone. 'Say' doesn't take a person directly — it uses 'say to someone', or no person at all.

She told her teacher that she had finished the test. · She said to her teacher that she had finished the test.

tell + особа (told him); say (to) + особа (said to him) — не «told to».

present simple → past simple

Maria said that she ___ (enjoy) long walks in the park.

Back: Maria said that she enjoyed long walks in the park., answer as enjoyed

After a past reporting verb like said or told, a present simple verb in direct speech moves back one tense to past simple.

He said that he worked in a bank. · They said that they lived near the station.

Present Simple → Past Simple: enjoys → enjoyed.

present continuous → past continuous

He told us that he ___ (wait) for the bus.

Back: He told us that he was waiting for the bus., answer as was waiting

Present continuous (is/am/are + -ing) in direct speech backshifts to past continuous (was/were + -ing) in reported speech.

She said that she was cooking dinner. · They told me that they were planning a trip.

Present Continuous → Past Continuous: is waiting → was waiting.

present perfect → past perfect

She said that she ___ (visit) Paris twice.

Back: She said that she had visited Paris twice., answer as had visited

Present perfect (have/has + past participle) in direct speech backshifts to past perfect (had + past participle) in reported speech.

He told me that he had paid the bill. · They said that they had already left.

Present Perfect → Past Perfect: has visited → had visited.

past simple → past perfect

He said that he ___ (meet) her the day before.

Back: He said that he had met her the day before., answer as had met

A past simple verb in direct speech often backshifts one step further to past perfect, to show it happened before the reporting.

She said that she had called earlier. · They told us that they had arrived before the storm.

Past Simple → Past Perfect: met → had met.

will → would

She said that she ___ (help) us later.

Back: She said that she would help us later., answer as would help

'Will' in direct speech becomes 'would' in reported speech — the same backshift pattern as other modal verbs.

He said that he would call back. · They told us that they would arrive soon.

will → would: 'I will help' → he said he would help.

can → could

He told me that he ___ (swim) very well.

Back: He told me that he could swim very well., answer as could swim

'Can' in direct speech becomes 'could' in reported speech.

She said that she could cook well. · They told us that they could finish on time.

can → could: 'I can swim' → he said he could swim.

have to / has to → had to

She said that she ___ (have to) work late.

Back: She said that she had to work late., answer as had to

'Have to' or 'has to' in direct speech becomes 'had to' in reported speech, just like other present forms moving into the past.

He told us that he had to leave early. · They said that they had to change their plans.

have to / has to → had to: завжди одна форма в минулому.

am/is/are going to → was/were going to

She told me that she ___ (be) going to start a new job.

Back: She told me that she was going to start a new job., answer as was

'Am/is/are going to' in direct speech becomes 'was/were going to' in reported speech — only the 'be' part changes.

He said that he was going to call back. · They told us that they were going to move house.

am/is/are going to → was/were going to: змінюється лише be.

pronouns and possessives change too

Maria said that ___ (my/her) phone was out of battery.

Back: Maria said that her phone was out of battery., answer as her

Pronouns and possessives change to match who is speaking now, not who originally spoke. Maria said 'my phone' — but when we report her words, it becomes 'her phone'.

Tom said that his brother had called him. · They said that their flight had been delayed.

Займенники теж змінюються: «my phone» (Марія) → her phone, коли переказуємо.
